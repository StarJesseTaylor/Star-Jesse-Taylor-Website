📍 Los Angeles 📅 Monthly 🧘 In Person

What this is.

I want to meet the people in LA who have been doing this work with me. Not on Zoom. Not through a screen. In a room together, sitting with what comes up, practicing what we have been teaching.

Once a month I host a small in-person meetup. We meditate. We share. We connect. Then we go back to our lives.

  • An hour or so of guided meditation and presence
  • Time to be in the room with people doing the same work as you
  • Optional sharing, never required
  • Open to anyone who has been touched by the work, no membership needed
