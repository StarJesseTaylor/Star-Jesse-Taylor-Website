You are not broken.

No one told you the truth. You do not have to fix yourself before you can live. You have been treating your life like a waiting room.

Every time you fight a thought, you feed it.

Your brain speaks one language. Engagement. Fight the anxiety and you train it to send more. The more you fight, the more you get.

It is a fire. Your attention is the firewood.

You kept feeding it for years without knowing. Stop adding wood and, in time, it runs out of oxygen.

So you stop fighting and walk the other way.

You stop asking how to fix yourself. You start asking what you want to grow. Your attention goes to your life instead of the fire.

You leave the fire and grow a garden.

Watered with sunshine through small daily actions. It grows over time. One day it is the thing holding you up.
