You see everyone moving forward.

They are not avoiding their passion. They are not staying in the safe job. They are not playing small in the room. Why do they flow through life so easily?

The difference

They do not take their thoughts and emotions as seriously as you do. When something intense comes up they say, "Oh, what a weird thing. Whatever. Let's keep moving forward with our valued actions."

But you take them seriously

  • "What does that mean about me?"
  • "What if this anxiety never goes away?"
  • "What if I'm losing my mind?"
  • "Why am I like this?"
  • "What if I never feel normal again?"

The brain's language is engagement.

By engaging, you showed your brain you love the battlefield. So the brain gives you more of the battlefield.

The more you fight, the more you get.

You need to stop fighting. Get off the battlefield. Walk in a different direction. That is how you show your brain you do not care about fighting anymore.

Stop asking the questions that keep you stuck

  • How do I fix this?
  • What's wrong with me?
  • What if it gets worse?
  • What if these thoughts never go away?

Start asking the questions that move you forward

  • What do I want to grow?
  • What is my excitement?
  • What would I do if I were already confident?
  • How would I act if I did not have these thoughts?

Your brain is a question-answering machine.

Ask the questions that move you in a healthy direction. Once you have the answer, you navigate your life that way.

The battlefield is a fire.

Your engagement is the firewood. Stop feeding it and, in time, the fire runs out of oxygen. The oxygen is the engagement.

Walk away from the fire and grow a garden.

A garden you nurture with water and sunshine through your actions. A garden that grows over time and ends up supporting you.

Then you live carefree.

You follow your passions. You walk into a room as yourself. You have conversations being yourself. No more head on the battleground.
