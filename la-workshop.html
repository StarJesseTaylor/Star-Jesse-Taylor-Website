Why In Person

Online is the trailer. The room is the movie.

You know me from social media. You have watched the videos. You may have read the book. So why come to Los Angeles?

Online I have 60 seconds to give you a teaching. In the room I have a full day. Online I am editing for the algorithm. In the room I am present with the person in the third row whose hand is shaking. Online you take in a clip and scroll on. In the room you do the work, you feel the work, and you walk out different.

In person, embodied learning creates deeper memory imprints than passive video. You will remember what happens in this room six months from now. You will not remember what you watched yesterday. People remember they went somewhere. They remember the room. They go home and use the tools more in the months that follow, because the day is encoded in their body, not just in their feed history.

What You Get In The Room

A full day immersive intensive.

Not a lecture. A practice container. Here is the shape of the day.

The Foundation Work

Most self help is a carpet over a hole. We build the floor first. You learn the difference between coping and rewiring, and exactly why the techniques you have tried did not hold.

The Brain Algorithm, Live

The penguin and elephant work, live, with your real brain in real time. We map the patterns running you and you learn to switch the channel. Most people say it is the first time they actually understood their own anxiety.

The No Brain Method In Practice

The exact tool from the book, practiced in the room until your body knows it. By the time you leave you can run this on yourself the next morning when the anxiety wakes up before you do.

The Value Garden Build

You build your own Value Garden in the room, the tool that becomes your daily compass when the brain throws noise at you. You leave with the actual document filled out in your handwriting.

Live Coaching And Hot Seat Work

I take real questions from real participants. Hot seat work for those who raise their hand. Your specific brain, in conversation with mine. This is the part you cannot get from any video, book, or course.

Surrender And Following Your Excitement

The difference between giving up and letting go. How to follow your excitement without losing your foundation. How to live in trust instead of in fear.

An Atmosphere That Charges You Up

Most people live in environments that drain them. We work on the practical changes to your environment and daily flow so your atmosphere supports your healing instead of fighting it.

Integration And Your 30 Day Plan

We integrate. You leave with a one page action plan written in your own hand, built around your specific patterns and your specific life. Not a slide deck. Your actual plan.

This Time, A Step Further

The Audacity Challenge.

We do not just teach in a room. At some point in the day, we walk out into the world together and do a real valued action that scares you. My whole philosophy, made physical. Stop talking about it, go live it, together, that day.

This is the part nobody else does. It is where the tools stop being ideas and become something your body has actually done. You leave not just knowing what to do, but having already done the thing you were afraid of, with the room behind you.

  • You have read the books, taken the courses, tried the apps. Some of it helped a little. None of it held.
  • You are ready to do the work in the room, not just watch
  • You can travel to Los Angeles on Saturday, November 14

This is not for you if…
