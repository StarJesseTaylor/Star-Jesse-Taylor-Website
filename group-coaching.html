Who this is for

You are already living your life. You are just not getting better.

You go to work. You see people. From the outside nothing looks wrong. But you are spending most of the day inside your head, checking how you feel, watching for the next thought, managing something nobody else can see.

You have probably read the books. You have probably done therapy, maybe for years. It helped and it did not finish the job, and part of you has started to wonder whether you are one of the people this does not work for.

You are not. You have been doing the right things with your attention in the wrong place.

What's inside

What you actually get.

Weekly live group coaching with me

Not a pre-recorded course. A live call every week where I teach the practice and then work with whatever is actually showing up for the people in the room that week.

Every call recorded

Miss one and you watch it back on your own time. Members have had their biggest realisations from replays, not from being live. Nothing is lost if your week goes sideways.

A room of people doing the same thing

People who do not need the problem explained to them. You post a win, someone who understands exactly what it cost you replies. That is most of why people stay.

Your progress, written down

Members keep a progress log in the community. On the days it feels like nothing is changing, you scroll back and see what you could not do a month ago.

My app, not available to the public

The practice, built into something you carry. Community members get access to it.

It all lives on Skool

One place for the calls, the recordings, the feed and the people. It works on your phone and in a browser, and you can cancel from inside your account whenever you want.
